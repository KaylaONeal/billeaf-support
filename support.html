Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to connect a bank card or log in?
Never. Billeaf stores 100% of your subscription ledger locally inside your device sandbox.

How do I export or import my subscriptions?
Tap Export CSV or open the actions menu and choose Export Ledger as CSV (Free), Export Ledger as JSON (Free), or Import CSV / JSON (Free). CSV & JSON portability is always free.

How do I restore Billeaf Pro on my Mac, iPhone, or iPad?
Open Settings or the Billeaf Pro sheet and tap Restore Purchases using the same Apple Account.
